# Digging In

A rifleman in a trench is worth five in the open. Today you dig a line, man it, wire it, and hold it.

## Your orders

- Dig a line of six trench pieces east of the base.
- Put nine men into the trench.
- Lay six pieces of wire in front of it.
- Beat off two attacks.

## Good to know

- Field works are on the sidebar's Defend tab. Click one, then drag a line on the ground.
- Three men fit in each trench piece. They stay in until told to Evacuate.
- Wire slows infantry to a crawl; tank traps stop vehicles.
