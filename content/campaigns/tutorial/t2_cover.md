# Cover and Armour

Men in the open die. Men in cover live, and keep firing. This lesson puts your section into a village and sends an enemy platoon, and then a tank, at it.

## Your orders

- Put at least four men inside the cottages.
- Line the rest up behind the sandbags.
- Knock out the tank with the PIAT teams that come up, and the 6-pounder in the village.

## Good to know

- Right-click a building with infantry selected to send them inside.
- Tanks shrug off rifle fire. Anti-tank weapons do far more harm to the side and rear armour than to the front.
- Evacuate (E) brings men out of a building or a trench.
