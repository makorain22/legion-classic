# First Orders

Welcome to the training ground. Before you command a battalion you will command a section: six riflemen and a Bren gun team.

## Your orders

- Select your section and march it to the farm by the well.
- Clear an enemy patrol out of the wood beyond it.
- Hold the wood when they come back for it.

## Good to know

- Drag a box with the left mouse button to select; right-click the ground to move.
- **A**, then a click: an attack-move. Your men fight whatever they meet on the way.
- Space pauses the game at any time. Hints appear at the top of the screen as you go.
