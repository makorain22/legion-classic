# Base and Supply

Armies run on supplies. In this last lesson you start with nothing but a Construction Lorry and four riflemen, and finish with a fighting force.

## Your orders

- Deploy the lorry into a Construction Yard.
- Build a power station and a post office.
- Build a Drill Hall and train ten riflemen.
- Build a logistics warehouse.
- Take the logistics point east of your yard.
- Build a Tank Workshop and a Cromwell.
- Destroy the enemy outpost to the north-east.

## Good to know

- Three buildings earn supplies every minute: the Post Office a little, the Logistics Warehouse more, the Strategic Fuel Depot most. Each construction yard allows three, two and one of them.
- A logistics point (a gold square on the minimap) is taken by holding it alone for twelve seconds. An office goes up, and three lorries carry money from it to your yard for as long as they and the office survive.
- Short of power, everything builds at half speed. Watch the power bar at the top of the sidebar.
- Engineers repair vehicles and buildings: right-click what needs mending.
