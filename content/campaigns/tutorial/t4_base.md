# Base and Supply

Armies run on supplies. In this last lesson you start with nothing but a Construction Lorry and four riflemen, and finish with a fighting force.

## Your orders

- Deploy the lorry into a Construction Yard.
- Build a power station and a supply dump on a supply point.
- Build a Drill Hall and train ten riflemen.
- Build a Tank Workshop and a Cromwell.
- Destroy the enemy outpost to the north-east.

## Good to know

- A Supply Dump only earns supplies standing on a supply point: the stacks of crates on the ground.
- Short of power, everything builds at half speed. Watch the power bar at the top of the sidebar.
- Engineers repair vehicles and buildings: right-click what needs mending.
