# Tripoli

**Tripoli, November 1946.** The Reich's last stronghold in Africa. Its heavy guns are being loaded onto ships in the harbour; if they sail, they will be in Sicily within a week.

The 7th Armoured Brigade attacks from the east, Kampfgruppe Sonnenberg from the south.

## Orders

- Destroy the four anti-tank guns on the ditch.
- Clear the harbour quarter.
- Keep Lieutenant-Colonel Ashworth alive.
- Optional: take the coastal battery before the ships sail.
- Optional: destroy the Festung Tripolis base.

## Intelligence

- The ditch is a line of tank traps east and south of the city, with four guns and bunkers behind it.
- The coastal battery stands by the harbour. The ships sail in twelve minutes.
- Sonnenberg's group will attack the ditch from the south as soon as you begin.

> The day the Reich lost Africa.
