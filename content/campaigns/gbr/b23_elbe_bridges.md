# The Elbe Bridges

**Magdeburg, April 1948.** Kampfgruppe Sonnenberg has seized all three bridges over the Elbe. Between it and the British lies the Magdeburg garrison, and Party divisions are coming from the east to retake the bridges.

## Orders

- Break the garrison's line through the city.
- Reach the city bridge before Sonnenberg is overrun.
- Hold the east bank with Sonnenberg.
- Keep Lieutenant-Colonel Ashworth alive; Sonnenberg must live.
- Optional: destroy the garrison base.

## Intelligence

- The garrison holds a trench through the city with bunkers and guns at each road, and three tanks behind it.
- Sonnenberg can hold out for twelve minutes. The Party divisions will attack the east bank twice before you arrive, and with heavy tanks after.
- If Sonnenberg owes you for Tripoli, he has more men on the bridges.

> "Somewhere beyond, the Russians. Let's not meet them today." - Lieutenant-Colonel Ashworth
