# The Gap at Hellenthal

**The Eifel, October 1947.** The brigade has come north to the West Wall. Between the rivers lies Hellenthal, a ruined industrial town, and behind it the only gap through the hills. A party of Kampfgruppe Sonnenberg has slipped into the woods behind the town.

## Orders

- Take the west of the town.
- Seize Hellenthal bridge.
- Hold the gap against the counter-attack.
- Destroy Kampfgruppe Hellenthal.
- Keep Lieutenant-Colonel Ashworth alive.

## Intelligence

- The town has bunkers, a gun, machine-gun nests, Panzerschreck teams and three tanks. A battery behind it has the range of your start line.
- Sonnenberg's men will silence the battery once you are into the town.
- If the gunners of Point 64 came out with their guns, two Sextons are with you. If Lantern burned their fuel, their attacks will be slow and Brigade has sent supplies. If Sonnenberg owes you for Tripoli, he has brought more men.

> In the German campaign, Sonnenberg's party is in the woods behind the town tonight. This is the other side of the same night.
