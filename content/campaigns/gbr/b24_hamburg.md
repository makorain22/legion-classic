# Hamburg

**Hamburg, May 1948.** Yesterday evening the regent ordered all German forces to cease fire. In Hamburg the Gauleiter's diehards did not listen: they hold the harbour and the flak tower. No. 15 Commando lands in the port.

## Orders

- Secure the landing quay.
- Take the harbour.
- Destroy the flak tower.
- Keep Major Munro alive.

## Intelligence

- Bunkers and machine-gun nests line the harbour. The flak tower stands north of it, with bunkers, riflemen and tanks.
- You have a construction truck ashore. The diehards will counter-attack from the north and the east.
- If Denmark rose, Danish volunteers have come to fight beside you. If Rotterdam's port was saved, landing craft from it have brought another company.

> "Last show, sir?" "God willing."
