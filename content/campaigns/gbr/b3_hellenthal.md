# The Gap at Hellenthal

**Rhineland, October 1947.** The Brigade has come north. Between the rivers lies Hellenthal, a ruined industrial town, and behind it the only gap through the hills.

## Orders

- Build up, push through the town, and destroy Kampfgruppe Hellenthal.

## Intelligence

- The Germans hold the east of the town with bunkers, an anti-tank gun and machine-gun nests, and will counter-attack hard.
- The streets favour infantry and anti-tank teams. Send the Firefly where the heavy armour is.
- If the gunners of Point 64 came out with their guns, they are here to support you.
