# The Docks at Antwerp

**Antwerp, September 1944.** After Normandy the Allies have raced across France into Belgium. In Germany, officers have tried to kill Hitler and failed; nobody yet knows what that will mean. Antwerp's docks are the greatest port in Europe, and the garrison's demolition teams are on the quays.

## Orders

- Take the docks before the demolition teams blow them.
- Hold the docks against the counter-attack.
- Keep Major Kerr alive.
- Optional: destroy the garrison base.

## Intelligence

- You have seven minutes. Bunkers, a gun and riflemen guard the quays; the demolition teams are among the cranes.
- If the docks are blown, the port is lost for months - but the river must still be held.
- Counter-attacks will come from the north, then the south.

> "The whole port, and the Jerries haven't blown it yet." - Major Thomas Kerr
