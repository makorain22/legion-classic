# Operation Lantern

**Libya, April 1946.** The enemy's armour cannot move without fuel. His three forward supply dumps lie forty miles behind the line, lightly guarded.

Lantern Force - carriers, Crusaders, two Dingos and a section of infantry - is going to burn them, and come home.

## Orders

- Destroy the northern, eastern and southern supply dumps.
- Bring Lantern Force home to the rendezvous.
- Keep Lieutenant-Colonel Ashworth alive.

## Intelligence

- Each dump has a machine-gun nest and a handful of riflemen. The eastern one also has a bunker, the southern one an anti-tank gun.
- Once a dump goes up, their tanks will come out of the camp in the south-east, and more will chase you home.
- There is no base and no reinforcement. What you have is what you bring home.

> "And if their armour wakes up, sir?" "Then we're faster than it is."
