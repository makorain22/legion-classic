# Fortress Antwerp

**Antwerp, December 1945.** For a month the regency has thrown everything at Antwerp: storms against the old forts, flying bombs on the docks. The Wessex Rifles hold the ring.

## Orders

- Hold Antwerp: keep the docks for twenty-five minutes, until the siege breaks.
- Keep Major Kerr alive.
- Optional: keep all three forts.

## Intelligence

- Seven storms are expected against the forts from the east, south and north, the later ones with heavy tanks.
- Flying bombs will fall on the docks and the city every few minutes.
- If ten of their units reach the docks, the port is lost.
- If the docks were taken intact in 1944, they keep earning; if the Walcheren batteries were silenced, convoys come up the Scheldt with supplies.

> "Frozen. Good. Let it stay frozen a while." - Major Kerr
