# Narvik

**Narvik, March 1947.** Every winter the Reich's Swedish iron ore comes down the mountain railway to Narvik and leaves by sea. No. 15 Commando lands at dawn on the east shore of the fjord, while the town is still asleep.

## Orders

- Take Narvik harbour.
- Take the ore quay.
- Hold Narvik against the mountain troops.
- Keep Major Munro alive.

## Intelligence

- The harbour is held by bunkers, a machine-gun nest, a gun and riflemen; the quay beyond it by a few more.
- Once the quay is taken, the Gebirgsjäger will come down off the heights in three waves, the second with light tanks from the north.
- You have a construction truck ashore. Snow slows everything that moves.

> "Ice on the rigging and the whole town still asleep." - Major Munro
