# The Knightsbridge Box

**Gazala, June 1946.** The Reich's panzer division has come round the southern end of the Gazala line. The brigade holds the Knightsbridge box - a ring of wire, tank traps, trenches and guns - while the line is re-formed behind it.

## Orders

- Hold the box for twenty minutes.
- Then break out to the east.
- Keep Lieutenant-Colonel Ashworth alive.

## Intelligence

- Expect six attacks from the west, the south and the north, with Tigers in the later ones. Their howitzers will shell the box.
- If twelve of their units get inside the box, it is broken.
- If Operation Lantern burned their fuel, two of those attacks will never come, and Brigade has sent extra supplies.

> The cork in the bottle.
