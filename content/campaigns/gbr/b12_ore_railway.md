# The Ore Railway

**Ofoten, April 1947.** The commandos hold the Ofoten railway from Narvik to the Swedish border. The Reich's mountain troops know every rock above it and are coming down to take it back.

## Orders

- Hold the railway for fifteen minutes, until the Norwegian battalions arrive. Do not lose two of the three stations.
- Keep Major Munro alive.

## Intelligence

- Each station has sandbags, a machine-gun nest, riflemen and an anti-tank team. Attacks will come from the heights north and south of each, again and again.
- A station is lost when the enemy holds it with nobody of yours left inside.
- If Narvik was held, two guns taken there are on a flatcar behind you.

> "We can lose one station. Not two." - Major Munro
