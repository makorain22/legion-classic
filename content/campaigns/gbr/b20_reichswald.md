# The Reichswald

**The German border, December 1947.** The last forest before Germany. The Wessex Rifles and the 2nd Canadian Division fight through the Reichswald in mud and snow to the bunkers of the West Wall on its far edge.

## Orders

- Break into the forest.
- Clear the heart of the forest.
- Break the West Wall.
- Keep Major Kerr alive.

## Intelligence

- Paratroopers hold strongpoints at the forest edge and in its heart, with bunkers, a machine-gun nest, a gun and snipers.
- The West Wall is a line of tank traps, a trench and bunkers every eighteen tiles, with tanks behind. The medium guns will fire on it when you reach it.
- The Canadians will advance on the north flank.

> "German soil. Four years from Antwerp." - Major Kerr
