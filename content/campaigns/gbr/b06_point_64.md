# Point 64

**Cyrenaica, March 1946.** The war in the desert should have ended in 1943. It did not: the Reich still holds Tripolitania. Now the 7th Armoured Brigade is pulling back to the Gazala line, and someone has to hold the ridge at Point 64 while it does.

And Brigade has passed on a strange warning: a German column may try to come over to the British lines.

## Orders

- Hold Point 64 for twelve minutes.
- Keep Lieutenant-Colonel Ashworth alive.
- Optional: bring two of the three six-pounders out intact.

## Intelligence

- Three attacks are expected from the east track: Panzer IVs first, then more with infantry and a howitzer, and finally Tigers.
- The column in the north wadi is Oberstleutnant Sonnenberg's - Afrika Korps veterans answering Rommel's call from London. Hold your fire when they come in. The Reich will be right behind them.
- You have three six-pounders dug in, two Churchills, a Sexton, infantry and two Royal Engineers.

> The day the Afrika Korps came over. The German campaign tells it from the other side of the wadi.
