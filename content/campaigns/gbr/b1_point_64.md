# Point 64

**Libya, March 1946.** The war in the desert should have ended in 1943. It did not. Now the Brigade is pulling back to the Gazala line, and someone has to hold the ridge at Point 64 while it does.

## Orders

- Hold Point 64 for twelve minutes.
- Optional: bring two of the three six-pounders out intact.

## Intelligence

- Three attacks are expected from the east track: Panzer IVs first, then more with infantry and a howitzer, and finally Tigers.
- A light column may try to work round through the wadi to the north.
- You have three six-pounders dug in, two Churchills, a Sexton, infantry and two Royal Engineers to keep the guns firing.
