# Epilogue: Lüneburg Heath

**Lüneburg Heath, June 1948.** The regency has fallen. The Provisional German Government, back in Berlin, is sending a delegation under General Heinrici to ask the Western Allies for an armistice. The Wessex Rifles are its escort across the heath.

## Orders

- Clear the road to the windmill, then the wood.
- Bring the delegation to British headquarters.
- Keep Major Kerr and the delegation alive.

## Intelligence

- Werwolf diehards are hiding in the heather. They will ambush the road from the south and the north, with Panzerfausts and at least one tank.
- The delegation's cars move up only when the road ahead is clear.

> "So that's how it finishes. A tent on a heath, and a pen." - Major Kerr
