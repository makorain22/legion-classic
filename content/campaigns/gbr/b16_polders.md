# The Polders

**The Betuwe, September 1947.** Between the Waal and the Lower Rhine lies the Betuwe: flat land below the water, held dry by dykes and pumps. The Reich has opened the sluices.

## Orders

- Take Pumping station Lingewaard, then Pumping station Bemmel.
- Take Elst.
- Keep Major Kerr alive.

## Intelligence

- The polders between the dykes are flooding. Men can wade them at half pace; vehicles must keep to the dyke roads.
- Each pumping station stands on its own mound, held by a bunker, a machine-gun nest and riflemen. Take one, and the land on its side of the cross dyke will be pumped dry.
- Elst has bunkers, a gun and two tanks; their base is behind it. Counter-attacks will come along the north and south dykes.

> "Whoever holds the pumps decides where the water goes." - Major Kerr
