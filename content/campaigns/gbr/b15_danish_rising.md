# The Danish Rising

**The Little Belt, August 1947.** Denmark has risen. In Fredericia the resistance holds the station and the church with shotguns and hope, and the Germans are coming back into the town. Beyond it, the Little Belt bridge joins Jutland to Funen.

## Orders

- Reach the resistance in Fredericia.
- Seize the west end, then the east end of the Little Belt bridge.
- Hold the bridge until the armour arrives from Esbjerg.
- Keep Captain Holloway alive; the resistance must not be wiped out.

## Intelligence

- You have recruits, a few riflemen, scout cars, a post office and a barracks. Recruit more as you go.
- Both ends of the bridge have bunkers; the west end has a gun, the east end two tanks.
- Counter-attacks will come from Funen across the bridge, and from the north.

> "And the Danes did half of it themselves." - Captain Margaret Holloway
