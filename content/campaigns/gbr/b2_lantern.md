# Operation Lantern

**Libya, April 1946.** The enemy's armour cannot move without fuel. Their three forward supply dumps lie forty miles behind the line, lightly guarded.

Lantern Force, carriers, Crusaders, two Dingos and a section of infantry, is going to burn them.

## Orders

- Destroy the northern, eastern and southern supply dumps.

## Intelligence

- Each dump has a machine-gun nest and a handful of riflemen. The eastern one also has a bunker, the southern one an anti-tank gun.
- Once a dump goes up, their tanks will come out of the camp in the south-east. Do not be there when they arrive.
- There is no base and no reinforcement. What you have is what you bring home.
