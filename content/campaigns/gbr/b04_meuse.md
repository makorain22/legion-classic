# The Meuse in Spring

**Maastricht, April 1945.** In Germany the regency has crushed the officers' civil war. Now it has turned west, with three panzer divisions on a twenty-mile front, and the Allied line is falling back through the Low Countries.

## Orders

- Hold Geleen, then fall back over the Maas before its bridge is blown.
- Hold Maastricht, then fall back over the Jeker before its bridge is blown.
- Hold Tongeren until the line steadies.
- Keep Major Kerr alive.

## Intelligence

- Each bridge is blown two minutes after the order to fall back. Whoever is still on the wrong side stays there.
- Both rivers have a ford far up or down stream; the enemy will find them eventually.
- If twelve of their units get into Tongeren, the line is broken.

> A costly spring. The line held from Antwerp to Tongeren, and no further east.
