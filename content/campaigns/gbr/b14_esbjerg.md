# Esbjerg

**Jutland, June 1947.** The landing on the Danish west coast. No. 15 Commando and a squadron of Churchills go in at Esbjerg, under three batteries in the dunes.

## Orders

- Silence Batteries A, B and C in the dunes.
- Take Esbjerg.
- Destroy the coast defence base.
- Keep Major Munro alive.

## Intelligence

- Each battery is a concrete casemate with a heavy gun, a bunker and riflemen. Tank traps line the dunes.
- The second wave - Cromwells and a rifle company - lands when the batteries are quiet. Until then you have only the beach and the dunes.
- A construction truck will come ashore once the town is taken. Expect a counter-attack down the east road.

> The coast of Denmark, and Denmark beyond it.
