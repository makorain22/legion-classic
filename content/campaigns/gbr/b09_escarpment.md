# The Escarpment

**The Gulf of Sirte, August 1946.** The British offensive has begun. Kampfgruppe Sonnenberg leads it west along the Via Balbia, the one road along four hundred kilometres of coast - and above the road, on the edge of the escarpment, the Reich's guns.

## Orders

- Silence the eastern and western guns on the escarpment.
- Take the pass.
- Hold the escarpment while Sonnenberg's column passes along the coast road.
- Keep Lieutenant-Colonel Ashworth alive.
- Optional: destroy the Sirte front base.

## Intelligence

- The brigade is on the plateau. The escarpment drops sheer to the coastal plain; only the pass climbs it.
- Each battery has a bunker and riflemen. The pass has bunkers, a gun and two tanks.
- Once the guns are silent, Sonnenberg's column will move. The Reich will counter-attack the pass from the south-west.
- If the gunners of Point 64 came out with their guns, two more Sextons are here.

> In the German campaign, Sonnenberg drives along the road below. Here you are the reason he can.
