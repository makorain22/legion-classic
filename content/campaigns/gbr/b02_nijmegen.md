# The Bridge at Nijmegen

**Nijmegen, September 1944.** An airborne corridor runs north through Holland towards Arnhem and the Rhine. Halfway along it, the great road bridge over the Waal at Nijmegen is still in German hands - and wired for demolition.

## Orders

- Take the south end of the bridge.
- Take the north end before the charges are fired.
- Hold the bridge until the Guards Armoured Division arrives.
- Keep Major Kerr alive.

## Intelligence

- Bunkers, a gun and a machine-gun nest hold the south end; more bunkers and two tanks the north end.
- Once the south end falls, they will think about the charges. You will have five minutes to get across.
- Kampfgruppe Henke will counter-attack the north end three times, the last with a Tiger and heavy guns.

> In this war the bridge beyond, at Arnhem, was never taken. Nijmegen is as far as the corridor goes.
