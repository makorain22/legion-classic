# The Ruhr

**Essen, January 1948.** Every gun barrel the regency has left is rolled in the Ruhr. Tonight Hanna Weiss's resistance cell has gone into the works, and Captain Holloway brings a British column to meet it.

## Orders

- Break into the steelworks.
- Reach Weiss's cell in the workers' quarter.
- Hold the quarter until Sonnenberg's column arrives.
- Take the Party headquarters.
- Keep Captain Holloway alive.

## Intelligence

- The works are held by bunkers, a machine-gun nest and riflemen. The Party headquarters is a fortified ministry with bunkers, tanks and their base behind it.
- The Party will storm the workers' quarter before you get there. Weiss's cell is in the buildings.
- Sonnenberg's column arrives six minutes after you reach the quarter.

> In the German campaign, Weiss waits in the quarter for Sonnenberg. This is who else came.
