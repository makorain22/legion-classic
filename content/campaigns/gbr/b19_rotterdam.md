# Rotterdam

**Rotterdam, November 1947.** Rotterdam has risen. The port garrison is wiring the quays - every crane, every warehouse - and the Dutch resistance's man in the harbour office says the charges go in nine minutes.

## Orders

- Clear the Merwehaven, the Maashaven and the Waalhaven before the charges are fired.
- Hold the port until the Commonwealth arrives.
- Keep Captain Holloway alive.

## Intelligence

- Each quay has a bunker, riflemen and a demolition party among the cranes. A quay is clear when you hold it with no enemy left on it.
- The resistance holds the city centre. You have recruits, riflemen, three tanks and a small base.
- Counter-attacks will come from the east and the west once the quays are settled.

> "The Dutch kept it. I just ran about." - Captain Holloway
