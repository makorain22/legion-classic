# The Trondheim Pens

**Trondheim, May 1947.** The U-boats that hunt the Arctic convoys shelter in a concrete pen at Trondheim, its roof too thick for any bomb. No. 15 Commando and the Norwegian resistance go in from the landward side to blow it from inside.

## Orders

- Meet the resistance at the farm west of the road.
- Take the dockyard gate.
- Get the sappers into the pens.
- Keep Major Munro and the sappers alive.

## Intelligence

- Milorg knows a way to the gate. Once you have met them, they will fight beside you.
- The gate has bunkers, a gun and riflemen; machine-gun nests cover the yard behind it. A flak tower stands east of the docks.
- After the gate falls, the garrison will counter-attack from the east and the west.

> "Five metres of concrete on the roof. No bomb will touch it. Sappers inside will." - Major Munro
