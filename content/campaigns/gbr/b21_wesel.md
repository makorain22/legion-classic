# The Rhine at Wesel

**Wesel, January 1948.** The Rhine. The 7th Armoured Brigade crosses in force at Wesel behind smoke and the heaviest barrage since Alamein, and the Free German Army crosses beside it.

## Orders

- Get across the Rhine and hold the far bank.
- Take Wesel.
- Hold the bridgehead against the counter-attack.
- Keep Lieutenant-Colonel Ashworth alive.
- Optional: destroy the Army Group H base.

## Intelligence

- There are two crossings, west and east. Each has bunkers and a gun on the far bank, and a trench runs the length of it.
- The Free German Army crosses at the eastern crossing.
- A construction truck will come over once Wesel is taken. The counter-attack will come from the north-east with heavy tanks.
- If Arnhem was taken, the corps has sent another squadron of tanks. If Sonnenberg owes you for Tripoli, the Free Germans have more tanks too.

> "Germans liberating Germany - it should be so."
