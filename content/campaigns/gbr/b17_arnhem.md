# Arnhem

**Arnhem, September 1947.** Three years ago the Wessex Rifles held Nijmegen bridge and listened to the airborne die at Arnhem, ten miles beyond. Now the battalion has come back to the road bridge over the Lower Rhine.

## Orders

- Take the south end of the bridge.
- Take the north end.
- Take Arnhem.
- Keep Major Kerr alive.
- Optional: destroy the garrison base.

## Intelligence

- Bunkers hold both ends of the bridge; the north end also has a gun and a machine-gun nest, with tanks and a Tiger in the town.
- Their heavy guns will shell the south end once you are on it. Counter-attacks will come down the north road and from the west.
- If the Guards held Nijmegen with you in 1944, they are here again.

> Three years late. Somebody put a flag on the bridge for the airborne.
