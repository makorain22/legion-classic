# Walcheren

**Walcheren, November 1944.** Antwerp is in British hands, but no ship can reach it while the batteries on Walcheren close the Scheldt. The RAF has holed the sea dyke at Westkapelle, and the sea is pouring in.

## Orders

- Silence Battery W15 on the north dune, Battery W13 on the south dune, and the Flushing battery.
- Keep Major Munro alive.

## Intelligence

- The island's interior is flooding. Men can wade it at half pace; nothing on wheels or tracks can cross it - including the enemy's reserves.
- Each battery is a concrete casemate with a heavy gun, a bunker, riflemen and a machine-gun nest. The guns will turn inland on the gap.
- Reserves will come from the east later, to save Flushing.

> "Half the island's under water. And we can wade." - Major Alasdair Munro
