# Berlin: The Ring

**Berlin, May 1948.** Gruppe Weiss holds the barricades in Wedding, with the men of every battalion that came over at Königs Wusterhausen. Kampfgruppe Adler breaks in from the east; Sonnenberg and the British are coming up from the south-west.

## Orders

- Break through the S-Bahn ring at Ostkreuz.
- Destroy the Friedrichshain flak tower, and link up with Gruppe Weiss.
- Take Tempelhof airfield with Sonnenberg and the British.
- Hold against the regency's last reserve.

## Intelligence

- The flak tower's guns cover the streets from Ostkreuz to Wedding.
- The regency's reserve is forming in the centre: every tank it has left.

> "I last saw Berlin in 1943, on leave. I don't recognise a street." - Major Adler
