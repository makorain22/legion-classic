# The Elbe Bridges

**Magdeburg, April 1948.** The British and Kampfgruppe Sonnenberg race for Berlin from the west. At Magdeburg the Reich has wired the three bridges over the Elbe for demolition.

## Orders

- Seize the north bridge, the city bridge and the south bridge before they are blown. At least one must be saved.
- Hold the bridges until the British arrive.
- Take the garrison's base on the east bank.

## Intelligence

- The north bridge's sappers are the quickest; the south bridge's the slowest.
- Every bridge we save means more British tanks in Berlin.
- Sonnenberg's Africa veterans are with us.

> Every bridge saved is a division in Berlin a day sooner.
