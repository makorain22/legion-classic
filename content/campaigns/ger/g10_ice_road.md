# The Ice Road

**The frozen steppe, January 1947.** The army group is crossing the steppe north towards the Donets, and every lorry goes through the crossroads at Verkhny. The Soviets know it.

## Orders

- Hold the crossroads while the columns pass.
- Raid the Soviet stores to the east, if the chance comes.
- Then follow the columns north over the frozen lake.

## Intelligence

- The ice will take lorries and tanks, one at a time. The Soviet guns will have its range.
- If the Tiger survived Kalach, it is with us.

> Two hundred lorries, eleven thousand men, and one crossroads.
