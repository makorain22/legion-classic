# Airlift

**Bohemia, December 1947.** Thirty thousand coalition soldiers are besieged at Eger. The only door is the airfield, and the British fly in what they can: three runs tonight.

## Orders

- Keep the airfield open for three supply flights.
- Gather the drops that scatter when the last flight cannot land.
- Destroy the four siege batteries.

## Intelligence

- Money is short. If the Drohobych refinery came through intact, we have fuel for the workshops.
- The siege guns sit in four batteries around the pocket, each with a small guard.

> When the guns are silent, the Dakotas can land again.
