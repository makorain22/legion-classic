# The Last Fuel

**Galicia, November 1947.** The army group came home to the coalition's lines and found them starving. Forty tanks, and fuel for six. The Reich holds the oil fields at Drohobych and the refinery behind them.

## Orders

- Seize the oil wells. Only then can the panzers be fuelled.
- Take the refinery intact.
- Hold the oil fields against the Reich and the Soviets.

## Intelligence

- Until the wells are ours, only infantry and light vehicles can be sent into battle.
- If the Reich thinks it will lose the refinery, it will set it on fire.
- A Soviet raiding force is coming over the Carpathians for the same prize.

> Every jerrican from Drohobych will be counted this winter.
