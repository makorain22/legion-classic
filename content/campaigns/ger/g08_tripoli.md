# Tripoli

**Tripoli, November 1946.** The Reich's last stronghold in Africa. Its heavy guns are being loaded onto ships in the harbour; if they sail, they will be in Sicily within a week.

## Orders

- Destroy the four anti-tank guns on the ditch.
- If you can, take the coastal battery before the ships sail.
- Clear the harbour quarter.

## Intelligence

- The ditch runs across the whole southern approach. Its guns cover every crossing.
- The ships sail in eight minutes. The battery protects them.
- Men who come over to us in the harbour will follow Sonnenberg north, wherever he goes.

> When Tripoli falls, the Reich has lost Africa. It will not be the last thing it loses.
