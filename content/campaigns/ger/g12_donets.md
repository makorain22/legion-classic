# Breakthrough on the Donets

**The Donets, June 1947.** The Soviets hold the ridge above the river, and the army group must cross beneath it.

## Orders

- Break the ridge line.
- Destroy the Soviet base behind it.
- Hold the crossing until the army group is over.

## Intelligence

- Corps artillery will fire one barrage on the ridge at the start. Go in behind it.
- The Soviets will throw everything at the crossing once they understand what we are doing.

> "We break the ridge, or we die beneath it. There isn't a third way." - Major Adler
