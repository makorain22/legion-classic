# The Kessel

**Morozovsk, February 1947.** The Soviets have closed a ring round part of the army group. The wounded are being flown out from the airfield in old transports, three flights a night.

## Orders

- Hold the airfield while the wounded are flown out.
- Keep all three transports flying, if you can.
- Then break through the ring to the west and link up with the relief force.

## Intelligence

- The ring is thinnest in the west, where the relief force is attacking.
- Soviet fighters and guns will go for the transports on the runway.

> Every flight carries forty wounded men. Every one of them is someone's son.
