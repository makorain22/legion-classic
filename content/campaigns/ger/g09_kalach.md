# Kalach Crossing

**The Don, December 1946.** When the coup failed, Manstein's army group on the Don declared for the coalition, and the Reich cut it off. Two winters later it is still there, between the Reich and the Soviets, and it has to come home.

Major Konrad Adler's panzer battle group leads the way. The bridge at Kalach is the only crossing for three divisions.

## Orders

- Take Kalach bridge before the Soviets blow it.
- Take the crossroads village beyond it, and hold it.
- Destroy the Soviet base.

## Intelligence

- Soviet sappers are already on the bridge. Three minutes, perhaps.
- A Soviet heavy tank company waits east of the village.
- We have one Tiger. Keep it alive: there will not be another.

> "Home is eleven hundred kilometres west, and every one of them is held by someone who wants us dead." - Major Adler
