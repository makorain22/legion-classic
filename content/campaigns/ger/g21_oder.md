# The Oder

**The Oder, April 1948.** Berlin is rising, and Kampfgruppe Adler is on the wrong side of the river. There are no bridges, only two fords - and the old fortress at Küstrin watching both. The Soviets, who also want Berlin, are a day behind.

## Orders

- Clear the west bank of the Oder.
- Take Festung Küstrin.
- Hold the crossing while the army group's columns get over.

## Intelligence

- If the Drohobych refinery came through intact, we have fuel to spare.
- The Tiger from Kalach is with us, if it survived.
- When the Soviets come, they will come behind us, on both roads.

> Eighty kilometres to Berlin.
