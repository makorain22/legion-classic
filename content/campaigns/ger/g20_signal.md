# The Signal

**Königs Wusterhausen, March 1948.** Adolf Hitler is dead. The Party has not dared say so on the radio. Inside the regency, dissident officers - men who have lost faith in Speer, and in the war - are ready to turn. They will move when they hear the news. Not before.

## Orders

- Seize the great transmitter at Königs Wusterhausen. It must not be destroyed.
- Hold it while the broadcast goes out.
- If you can, take the garrison's barracks.

## Intelligence

- The officers' committee has its own men nearby, and three battalions in the garrison ready to come over.
- A Party panzer division is on the road from Berlin.

> The biggest transmitter in Germany. Everyone in the Reich will hear it.
