# Epilogue: The Mountain Road Home

**The Bavarian Alps, June 1948.** The regency has surrendered. Speer is under arrest; the Provisional Government sits in Berlin and has offered the Western Allies an armistice. On the Oder, the war with the Soviets goes on.

High above Berchtesgaden, a few hundred Party diehards refused the surrender. Bataillon Brenner, who fought the first battle of the coup, is sent to end the last.

## Orders

- Take the pass before the diehards' convoy gets through.
- Take the outer works.
- Storm the redoubt.

## Intelligence

- Every man who came over the mountain road in 1945 and survived the valley war is with us.
- Corps artillery will fire three shoots on the outer works.

> "Four years ago I took a telephone exchange for a coup that failed. Now let's go home." - Hauptmann Brenner
