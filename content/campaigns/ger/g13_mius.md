# The Mius Line

**The Mius, July 1947.** The army group has reached the Reich's own lines. Major Adler is going forward under a white flag, in his own car, to ask for passage home. The Soviets are a day behind.

Some of the men on the other side of the river fought beside us in 1943. Some of them will remember.

## Orders

- Drive Major Adler's car to the Reich command post under a white flag.
- Get him back to his men, whatever happens.
- If you can, take the battalion headquarters before the Reich's reserves arrive: its battalions may come over.
- Break through the Mius line to the road west.

## Intelligence

- Nobody knows what the Reich will answer.
- The Soviets will reach the river within the hour.

> The Mius is where Adler stopped fighting to get home and started fighting because he believed in it.
