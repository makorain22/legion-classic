# Walküre

**Berlin, 20 July 1944.** The bomb has gone off at the Wolf's Lair. Nobody yet knows whether Hitler is alive. In the Bendlerstrasse, the conspirators have issued the order every Replacement Army unit has been waiting for: *Valkyrie*.

Hauptmann Josef Brenner's battalion has been told it is putting down a Party putsch. Brenner knows better. He was told the truth a week ago, and said yes.

## Orders

- Take the signals exchange, and the radio mast beside it.
- Hold the exchange until the Bendlerstrasse confirms the coup.
- If it fails, break out through the rail yards to the lorries.

## Intelligence

- The exchange is guarded by a Feldgendarmerie company with machine guns in the forecourt.
- The guard battalion from Moabit will move within the hour, whichever way the wind is blowing.
- Brenner's scout car carries the battalion's only radio. Keep him alive.

> "If it works, we will be the men who ended it. If it doesn't, we will be the first men they hang." - Hauptmann Brenner
