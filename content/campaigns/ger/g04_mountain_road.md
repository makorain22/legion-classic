# The Mountain Road

**The Tyrol, April 1945.** It is over. The coalition's corps have surrendered one after another, and its officers are fleeing abroad. Oster is in London, forming a government in exile. Brenner has nowhere to go but up.

His battalion and the columns of refugees who follow it are climbing the last pass into the high valleys, where the Party's writ has never quite run. The loyalists are close behind.

## Orders

- Take the pass fort, and hold the road while the columns climb.
- Get a pioneer to the viaduct, and drop it behind us.
- Take the border post, and hold it until the last column is through.

## Intelligence

- The fort is held by a weak garrison of old reservists.
- The viaduct is the only way up for vehicles. Without it, the pursuit stops.
- Every man who reaches the valleys will fight again. Count them.

> "We'll be back. Not this year, and maybe not next. But we'll be back." - Hauptmann Brenner
