# The Government Quarter

**Berlin, May 1948.** The last act. Across the Landwehr canal lie the ministries of the regency: the Bendlerblock, where the officers of 1944 were shot; the Air Ministry; the Foreign Office; and behind them the Reich Chancellery, where the regent is said to be in the cellars.

## Orders

- Take a bridge over the Landwehr canal.
- Clear the Bendlerblock, the Air Ministry and the Foreign Office.
- Storm the Reich Chancellery.

## Intelligence

- Each ministry is a fortress, with bunkers and machine guns in its courtyards.
- The officers' committee will send its battalions in from the north-east once we are over the canal.
- Not every Party man will obey an order to surrender.

> Major Adler and Hanna Weiss fight this battle together. Neither may fall.
