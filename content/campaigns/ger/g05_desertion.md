# Desertion

**Libya, March 1946.** The war in Africa never ended. The Reich still holds Tripolitania against the British Eighth Army, and its old Afrika Korps veterans still man the line.

Then a message comes from London, in a voice every one of them knows: Erwin Rommel, now with the Provisional German Government, calls on his old soldiers to come over. Oberstleutnant Max Sonnenberg has made up his mind.

## Orders

- Bring three supply trucks to the fuel depot, and seize it.
- Break through the checkpoint on the coast road.
- Reach the British at Point 64, and hold it while they pull out.

## Intelligence

- Until we move, the Reich's units around us are still our comrades.
- The British have been warned we are coming. Their gunners have not all been told.
- Point 64 is under heavy attack. The British cannot hold it long.

> "Rommel never lied to us in the desert. I don't believe he is lying now." - Oberstleutnant Sonnenberg
