# Kalach Crossing

**December 1946.** The armistice of 1945 never came on the Don. Two winters later, the front still runs through the snow west of Stalingrad, and the bridge at Kalach is the only way home for three divisions.

The Soviets took the crossroads village east of the bridge last night. Kampfgruppe Adler, one Tiger, two Panzer IVs, two half-tracks and a company of grenadiers, is all that stands between them and the bridge.

## Orders

- Take the village at the crossroads.
- Hold it against the counter-attack that is sure to follow.
- Destroy the Soviet base on the east bank.

## Intelligence

- The village is held by riflemen with two bunkers, machine guns and anti-tank rifles, and one T-34.
- The Soviet base lies on the river bank to the east. Expect their armour to cross within ten minutes of the village falling.
- You have a construction truck. A base and a supply depot will keep the Kampfgruppe in the fight.

> Keep the Tiger alive. There will not be another one.
