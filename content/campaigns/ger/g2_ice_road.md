# The Ice Road

**January 1947.** Kalach held, but the bridge did not: the Soviets blew it behind you. The only way out now is across the frozen river, and the supply columns need time to cross.

Adler holds the crossroads at Verkhny, the one place the Soviets can cut the ice road.

## Orders

- Hold the crossroads for fifteen minutes.
- Keep at least one unit at the crossroads at all times.
- Optional: destroy the Soviet base. Its stores would be worth having.

## Intelligence

- Three assaults are expected from the east road, each heavier than the last. The third will include breakthrough tanks.
- Panzergrenadiers will come up the ice road after seven minutes.
- Your base behind the crossroads is already built. Use it.
