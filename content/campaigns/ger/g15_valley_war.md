# The Valley War

**The Tyrol, September 1947.** For two years Bataillon Brenner has held the high valleys: farmers by day, a battalion by night. The men who reached the valleys over the mountain road in 1945 are its core.

Now the Reich's mountain division is coming down from its fort on the heights. And the British, at last, are sending supplies - by parachute, into the open.

## Orders

- Hold the valley.
- Recover the British supply containers at drop zones Anton and Berta.
- Then take the fort on the heights.

## Intelligence

- The Gebirgsjäger will come at the valley mouth, and over the ridges.
- The drop zones are in the open. The Reich will go for the containers too.

> The coalition is short of everything except stubbornness.
