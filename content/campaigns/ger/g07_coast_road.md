# The Coast Road

**The Gulf of Sirte, August 1946.** The British offensive has begun, and Kampfgruppe Sonnenberg leads it west along the Via Balbia, the one road along four hundred kilometres of coast.

## Orders

- Take the pass through the escarpment.
- Take the airfield - with both hangars standing, if you can.
- Hold the road junction against the counter-attack.
- Storm Sirte harbour at the end of the road.

## Intelligence

- The pass is mined and covered by anti-tank guns.
- The RAF wants the airfield intact for its fighters.
- The Reich's armour reserve is somewhere behind Sirte.

> The Via Balbia: tarmac, sand, sea and nothing else, all the way to Tripoli.
