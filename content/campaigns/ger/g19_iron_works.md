# The Iron Works

**Essen, January 1948.** Every barrel for every gun the regency has left is rolled in the Ruhr. Albert Speer, who rules in the name of a dying Hitler, nationalised the works himself.

Hanna Weiss's resistance cell has friends on the night shift. Tonight it goes into the works.

## Orders

- Get a pioneer into the furnace hall.
- Knock out the power station.
- Then hold the workers' quarter until Sonnenberg's column arrives, and take the Party headquarters.

## Intelligence

- Gruppe Weiss has no base and no money: levies, a few pioneers, two snipers.
- When the furnaces go out, the workers will rise - and the Party will come for them.
- Kampfgruppe Sonnenberg is on its way from Hellenthal.

> "They call us the resistance. We are only the people who stopped saying yes." - Hanna Weiss
