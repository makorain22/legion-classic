# The Wells at Bir Sidra

**The Libyan desert, May 1946.** Sonnenberg's group now fights beside the British 7th Armoured Brigade. Together they will make a base deep in the desert, at the wells of Bir Sidra, and strike the Reich's forward airfield from it.

The Reich knows exactly where the water is.

## Orders

- Build a base at the wells.
- Beat off the raids that are sure to come.
- Destroy the forward airfield's hangars and aircraft.
- If you can, destroy the supply dumps as well.

## Intelligence

- Three raiding columns are expected, from the north and the west.
- The airfield is lightly guarded; its fighters are the danger.
- The dumps hold the fuel for the Reich's whole southern front.

> A base in the desert lives or dies by its water, and by its trucks.
