# The Dnieper Dam

**Zaporozhye, August 1947.** The great dam over the Dnieper is the only crossing for a hundred kilometres. The Reich holds its west end with orders to blow it; the Soviets are coming down the east bank.

## Orders

- Seize the west end of the dam before the charges go.
- Hold both ends while the army group crosses.
- Then get a pioneer to the middle of the dam - and drop it behind us.

## Intelligence

- The mutineers from the Mius are with us, if they came over.
- Reich and Soviet troops will attack from both banks at once.

> Across the dam, and the Dnieper is between us and the Soviets.
