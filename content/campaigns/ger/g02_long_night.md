# The Long Night

**The night of 20 July 1944.** Hitler survived. By midnight the coup is collapsing: Stauffenberg and his friends are being shot in the Bendlerblock courtyard, and the Gestapo has the lists.

One man must not be taken. Generalmajor Hans Oster, the conspiracy's oldest hand, has a seat on a transport waiting at Rangsdorf airfield. Bataillon Brenner is to put him on it.

## Orders

- Clear the roadblock on the forest road.
- Get the general across the river by the ford.
- Take the airfield and hold it until the transport is away.

## Intelligence

- The roadblock is held by Feldgendarmerie with a light tank.
- The bridge is down; the ford north of it is shallow but narrow.
- Loyalist troops will reach Rangsdorf from Berlin within the hour.

> If Oster reaches London, the coup has a government. If he doesn't, it was only a crime.
