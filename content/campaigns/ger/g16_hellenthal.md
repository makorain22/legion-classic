# The Gap at Hellenthal

**The Rhineland, October 1947.** The British are attacking the West Wall at Hellenthal. A small party of Kampfgruppe Sonnenberg has slipped behind the line, into the woods east of the town, to silence the guns that cover the gap.

It is the first time in four years that Sonnenberg has stood on German soil.

## Orders

- Silence the gun battery behind the town.
- Seize Hellenthal bridge as the British attack.
- Hold the gap against the counter-attack.

## Intelligence

- No base, no reinforcements - until the guns are quiet.
- Sonnenberg's Africa veterans will join us once the British attack goes in.
- The garrison's base south-east of the town will counter-attack the bridge.

> This mission is fought from the other side in the British campaign.
