# Brothers' War

**Silesia, October 1944.** The coup became a civil war. Half a dozen army corps declared for the conspirators; the Party called up its own divisions and the rest of the army stayed in barracks, waiting to see who would win.

Bataillon Brenner holds the western half of a mill town on the coalition's line. The loyalist Party division holds the other half, and its guns are parked behind it.

## Orders

- Hold our end of the town.
- Destroy the loyalist artillery park.
- Get the refugee columns out on the west road.

## Intelligence

- Panzer Abteilung Hollmann, a coalition tank battalion, is on its way from Glogau. It will come, but late.
- A heavy tank stands in the town square. It will not move until the Party orders it to.
- Thousands of civilians have taken shelter in the church and the mill. They will follow us when we go.

> Twenty-three thousand Germans will be killed by Germans before this winter is out.
