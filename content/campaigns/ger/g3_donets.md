# Breakthrough on the Donets

**June 1947.** Summer, and the front has moved again. The Soviets have dug a line along the ridge above the Donets: trenches, wire, guns behind. Their reserves are two days away. Adler has fifteen minutes before they start to arrive.

## Orders

- Break the line on the ridge.
- Destroy the Soviet base beyond it.

## Intelligence

- The ridge is held by a rifle battalion with machine guns and two T-34s, behind a belt of wire.
- Their base has bunkers, an anti-tank gun and a gun-howitzer emplacement.
- You have two construction trucks, a howitzer and the best armour the division could spare. Use the howitzer on the trenches before the tanks go in.
