# The Oboyan Road

**South of Kursk, October 1946.** The Reich has gathered what it has left in the south into one autumn offensive, and it is coming down the Oboyan road. The tank corps that will throw it back is fifteen minutes away.

## Orders

- Hold the Oboyan road for fifteen minutes.
- Hold the first line, then fall back to the second and the third when ordered.
- When the tank corps arrives, throw the enemy back to the first line.
- Keep Polkovnik Gromov alive.

## Intelligence

- Five waves are expected, each heavier than the last: Panzer IVs, grenadiers, half-tracks and later Tigers. Their heavy guns will shell the first line.
- The third line is the last. If eight of their units reach Oboyan, the road is lost.
- The rocket trucks are back by the second line. Engineers can mend the trenches between waves.

> "Not in the first line, maybe. But we'll hold it." - Polkovnik Gromov to General Rokossovsky
