# The Bryansk Forest

**Bryansk, November 1946.** Behind the German lines the forest belongs to the partisans by night. The railway to the front crosses three streams on three bridges, and every train to the Oboyan offensive came over them.

## Orders

- Blow the western, middle and eastern railway bridges.
- Then destroy the supply column before it reaches the depot.
- Keep Nadezhda Orlova alive.
- Optional: free the three villages; their men will take up rifles.

## Intelligence

- Each bridge has a machine-gun nest at one end and riflemen at the other. Engineers make short work of a bridge once the guards are down.
- The streams can only be crossed at the fords, far upstream and far downstream, or on the bridges themselves.
- Once the first bridge goes, the Feldgendarmerie will send hunter groups after the camp.
- There is no base and no reinforcement but the villages.

> "The forest has no front line. That is why it is ours." - Nadezhda Orlova
