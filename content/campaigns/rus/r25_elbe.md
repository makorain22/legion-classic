# Epilogue: The Elbe

**Torgau, May 1948.** Berlin has fallen and the regent is a prisoner. On the Elbe at Torgau, the 5th Guards Rifle Division has reached the end of its road west. On the far bank wait the British and the Free German Army.

On this bank, a handful of Party diehards still have not surrendered.

## Orders

- Clear the northern and southern strongpoints on the east bank.
- Meet the British and the Free Germans on the bridge.
- Keep Polkovnik Gromov alive.

## Intelligence

- The diehards are Volkssturm and riflemen, with bunkers, a gun and one tank.
- The British and the Free Germans are allies. Nobody fires across the river.
- If Major Adler was taken at Morozovsk, he is with the division, and will be handed back to his own side on the bridge.

> "Divided. Well. At least nobody's shooting." - Polkovnik Gromov
