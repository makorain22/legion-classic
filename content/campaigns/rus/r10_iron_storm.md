# Iron Storm

**Kirovsk, March 1947.** With the army group gone, the Reich's southern front rests on one fortress: Kirovsk, in the Donbas. Three years of concrete - a trench line, wire, tank traps, bunkers, guns and a heavy tank behind them all.

The Front has given the 61st Assault Engineer Brigade four rocket batteries for this attack.

## Orders

- Take the northern and southern strongpoints on the flanks.
- Break the main line.
- Destroy Festung Kirovsk.
- Keep Kapitan Nazarov alive.
- Optional: take the gun park behind the line with its heavy guns intact.

## Intelligence

- The defences face west; the flanks are weaker. Their heavy guns will fire on your start line.
- Rocket salvoes are inaccurate but devastating on trenches and wire. Engineers mend what the German guns damage.
- Once both flanks fall, the Tiger behind the line will come out.
- If the Sokolovo tank crews are still with the division, a new IS tank has been sent for them.

> "Concrete is only patience. We have more." - Kapitan Rustam Nazarov
