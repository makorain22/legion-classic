# The Bridgehead

**The Seym, September 1946.** The division has reached the river. Every bridge for twenty kilometres is down, but there are two fords. Once across, the far bank must be held until the engineers bring up a construction truck.

## Orders

- Get ten men or vehicles onto the far bank.
- Hold the bridgehead for eight minutes.
- Then build a base and destroy Kampfgruppe Brandt.
- Keep Polkovnik Gromov alive.

## Intelligence

- The west ford is by the village, the east one on the old ferry road. Infantry can wade where vehicles cannot; send the men first.
- Brandt has a screen of grenadiers, machine-gun nests and a gun on the far bank, and his base to the north. He will counter-attack as soon as you are across, and his howitzers will find the bank.
- If the Sokolovo tank crews came through, they are with you.

> The Seym is the last river before Kursk. After Kursk, the Don.
