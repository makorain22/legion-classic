# The Ring at Morozovsk

**Morozovsk, February 1947.** The ring round the army group is closed. Inside it, at Morozovsk airfield, three old transports fly out the wounded every night and fly in shells. Nadezhda Orlova's partisans have been watching the airfield all winter.

This is the night the ring has to hold.

## Orders

- Reach the two watch posts over the runway, west and south, so the brigade's guns can see.
- Destroy the three transports before the last flight.
- Hold the western ring when the army group breaks out.
- Keep Nadezhda Orlova alive.

## Intelligence

- The first transport takes off in five minutes. Each flight takes forty wounded out and brings supplies in.
- When the airlift ends, the army group will throw everything at the western ring, and its relief force will attack from outside. The 5th Shock Army's riflemen hold the trench line there.
- Major Konrad Adler commands inside the pocket. If the ring holds, the army group will have to ask for terms.
- If the ice road was cut, the pocket is short of fuel and its attacks will be weaker.

> In another telling of this war, the transports got away and the army group broke out. Not in this one.
