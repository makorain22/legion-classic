# The Chir

**The Chir river, December 1946.** When the coup of 1944 failed, Manstein's army group declared for the coalition, and the Reich cut it off on the Don. Yesterday its vanguard, Kampfgruppe Adler, took the bridge at Kalach. Now the whole army group is coming west along the Chir to go home - and a Reich column is coming east to finish it.

## Orders

- Seize the Chir bridge before the army group's vanguard reaches it.
- Hold the bridge: the army group must not cross.
- Then destroy the army group's vanguard on the east bank.
- Keep Podpolkovnik Zharkov alive.

## Intelligence

- A coalition picket of riflemen and an anti-tank team already holds the bridge. You have four minutes.
- The army group will attack from the east with Panzer IVs, a Tiger and tank destroyers. The Reich column will come down the west road soon after.
- The Reich and the coalition are enemies too. Let them fight each other where they meet.

> "Two German armies, both against us, both against each other. Hold the bridge." - Podpolkovnik Zharkov
