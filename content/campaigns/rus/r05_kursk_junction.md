# The Kursk Junction

**Kursk, November 1946.** The 17th Guards Tank Brigade has come up to the front. Kursk's railway junction is the key to the whole sector, and the Germans have made it a fortress facing west: guns, bunkers, a line of tank traps.

## Orders

- Go round by the southern track the partisans know.
- Take the Kursk junction.
- Hold it until the trains get through.
- Keep Podpolkovnik Zharkov alive.
- Optional: destroy Kampfgruppe Kursk's base in the east.

## Intelligence

- The southern track passes a hamlet held by a few riflemen and a machine-gun nest.
- Once the junction falls, the Germans will counter-attack from the east road, and later from the north-east with a heavy tank.
- If Orlova's partisans came through the forest, they will guide you and fight beside you.

> "Tanks don't go through fortresses, Comrades. They go round them." - Podpolkovnik Ivan Zharkov
