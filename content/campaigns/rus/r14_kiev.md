# Kiev

**Kiev, August 1947.** Kiev has been under the Reich for six years. The 61st Assault Engineer Brigade attacks from the bridgehead north of the city: every house on its streets is a strongpoint now.

## Orders

- Clear the northern districts.
- Take the Golden Gate square.
- Take the heights over the Dnieper, where the Reich's guns are.
- Keep Kapitan Nazarov alive.
- Optional: destroy the garrison base in the south-west.

## Intelligence

- The garrison's infantry is in the buildings. Engineers and snipers are the tools for streets; tanks die in them without infantry.
- The Golden Gate square is their keep, with bunkers at its corners, a gun and a Tiger.
- Their howitzers on the heights will shell the bridgehead.
- If the Dnieper dam was saved, four more T-34s have come across it.

> "District by district. Storm groups: engineers with every section." - Kapitan Nazarov
