# Berlin: The Outer Ring

**Berlin, April 1948.** The storm of Berlin has begun. The 61st Assault Engineer Brigade fights in from the east along the S-Bahn ring, district by district, under the guns of a flak tower that commands them all.

## Orders

- Take Lichtenberg.
- Take Neukölln.
- Take Tempelhof airfield.
- Destroy the flak tower.
- Keep Kapitan Nazarov alive.

## Intelligence

- The districts are garrisoned house by house, with bunkers and machine-gun nests at the crossings. Snipers and engineers are worth more here than tanks.
- The flak tower's guns reach the start line. It has two metres of concrete and two Tigers below it.
- If Warsaw was saved, Polish insurgents will join the attack on Neukölln. If the Narew road was cut, four more T-34s are with you.

> In the canon it was Kampfgruppe Adler fighting round this ring. Here it is the Red Army.
