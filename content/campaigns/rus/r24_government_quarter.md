# The Government Quarter

**Berlin, May 1948.** The last act. Across the Landwehr canal lie the ministries of the regency: the Air Ministry, the Foreign Office, the Reichstag, and behind them the Reich Chancellery, where the regent is said to be in the cellars.

## Orders

- Take a bridge over the Landwehr canal.
- Clear the Air Ministry and the Foreign Office, and take the Reichstag.
- Storm the Reich Chancellery.
- Hold it against the Party diehards.
- Keep Kapitan Nazarov alive.

## Intelligence

- Each ministry is a fortress, with bunkers and machine guns in its courtyards. A flak tower stands north of the quarter.
- The regency may surrender when the Chancellery falls. Not every Party man will obey.
- If the parley in the Spree forest succeeded, a Free German battalion will come in from the north-east.

> Every man in the Front wants to be the one who raises the banner on the Reichstag.
