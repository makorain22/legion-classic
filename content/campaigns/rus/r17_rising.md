# The Rising

**Warsaw, November 1947.** Warsaw has risen. The insurgents of the Home Army hold the old town against the whole garrison, and their radio has been calling for help for three days.

In the other war, the Red Army stood on this bank of the Vistula and watched the city die. Not in this one.

## Orders

- Cross the Vistula and reach the insurgents in the old town before it falls.
- Take the west ends of the northern and southern bridges.
- Hold the bridges until the Front crosses.
- Keep Nadezhda Orlova alive; the Home Army must not be wiped out.
- Optional: destroy the garrison base.

## Intelligence

- The water is low at the ferry crossing: men can wade it. Both bridges stand, held at their west ends by bunkers and guns.
- The old town can hold out for ten minutes. The garrison is attacking it now, and will send more.
- Once both bridges are yours, the garrison's armour will come for them from the south-west.
- If the Bryansk partisans are still with you, six more have come into the city.

> In this telling, Warsaw is not alone.
