# Winter Storm

**The Myshkova, March 1947.** The pocket at Morozovsk is starving, but it has not surrendered. To the west, the coalition has gathered its last armoured force on the Don - the relief force under Oberst Hoth - and it is driving east to break the ring from outside.

## Orders

- Hold the Myshkova for twenty minutes, until the pocket surrenders.
- Then destroy the relief force's base.
- Keep Podpolkovnik Zharkov alive.

## Intelligence

- The river can be crossed at three places: the main road and two tracks north and south. Each has trenches, two guns and a machine-gun nest on the east bank.
- Expect five attacks, the heaviest on the main road. Hoth has Tigers and will shell the river line.
- If ten of their units reach your base, the ring is broken.
- If the Chir crossing was held, two more rocket batteries are with you.

> Whatever the coalition has left on the Don is on the other side of this river.
