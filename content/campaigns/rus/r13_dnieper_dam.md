# The Dnieper Dam

**Zaporozhye, June 1947.** The great dam over the Dnieper is the only crossing for a hundred kilometres. In the war before this one it was blown twice. The Reich has wired the whole span again; a demolition team waits at the far end.

## Orders

- Take the east end of the dam.
- Take the west end before the charges are fired.
- Hold both ends of the dam.
- Keep Podpolkovnik Zharkov alive.
- Optional: destroy the garrison base.

## Intelligence

- The charges will be fired in ten minutes. Once the west end is yours, the demolition team is finished.
- The span is narrow and covered by a bunker and a gun at the west end.
- The garrison will counter-attack from the south-west and then the north, with a Tiger. If they bring the span down, the crossing is lost.

> Here the dam stands, and the Red Army crosses it. In the canon, the coalition dropped it behind them.
