# The Mius Line

**The Mius, May 1947.** The Reich has fortified the Mius for four years: three belts of trenches, wire, tank traps and bunkers, one behind the other, with heavy guns behind them all. The Front has gathered twelve hundred guns for the 5th Guards Rifle Division's attack.

## Orders

- Break the first, second and third belts.
- Then reach the road west.
- Keep Polkovnik Gromov alive.
- Optional: destroy the Reich base.

## Intelligence

- The barrage falls on the first belt as the attack begins, and lifts to the next belt as each one breaks.
- Each belt has bunkers every twenty tiles, two guns on the road and riflemen along its length. The third belt is the strongest.
- Expect counter-attacks from the north and down the road west after each belt falls.
- If the heavy guns of Kirovsk were taken intact, they fire for you today, and two more rocket batteries have joined.

> "Four years they built this. Let's see how long it takes us." - Polkovnik Gromov
