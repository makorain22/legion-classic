# Iron Storm

**February 1947.** Kirovsk. The Germans have made the town a fortress: a trench line, wire, tank traps, bunkers, guns and a heavy tank behind them all.

The Front has given the division four rocket truck batteries for this attack. Use them.

## Orders

- Destroy Festung Kirovsk.

## Intelligence

- The defences face west. Scouts report the north and south flanks are weaker.
- Rocket salvoes are inaccurate but devastating on trenches and wire. Engineers can repair what the German guns damage.
