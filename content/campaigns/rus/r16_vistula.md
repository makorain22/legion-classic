# The Vistula Bridgehead

**The Vistula, October 1947.** The 5th Guards Rifle Division and the 1st Polish Army crossed the Vistula in the night and hold six kilometres of its west bank. Three panzer divisions are coming to put them back in the river.

## Orders

- Hold the bridgehead for twelve minutes.
- Then break out and take Magnuszew.
- Keep Polkovnik Gromov alive.
- Optional: destroy the panzer corps base.

## Intelligence

- The panzer divisions will come one after another: down the north road, from the north-west, and from the north-east, each with heavy tanks. Their guns will shell the bridgehead.
- The Poles hold the left of the line; they will join the breakout.
- If ten of their units reach the riverbank, the bridgehead is lost.
- If the division came through the Pripet marshes, a fresh rifle company is with you.

> "It is our river." - Pulkownik Zawadzki, 1st Polish Army
