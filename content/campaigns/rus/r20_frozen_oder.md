# The Frozen Oder

**Küstrin, January 1948.** The Oder froze last week. Beyond it, eighty kilometres away, is Berlin; in front of it, on the west bank, the old fortress of Küstrin.

## Orders

- Cross the ice and hold the west bank.
- Hold the bridgehead until the sappers bring the tanks over.
- Take Festung Küstrin.
- Keep Polkovnik Gromov alive.
- Optional: destroy the fortress base.

## Intelligence

- The ice carries men, not tanks. Küstrin's howitzers will shell it; where a shell lands, the ice breaks and whoever is on it goes through. Spread out.
- The pontoon bridge takes six minutes once the west bank is held. Expect counter-attacks from the north, with a Tiger.
- The fortress has bunkers, guns and machine-gun nests round it.
- If the guns of Posen were taken, two more batteries are with you.

> In the canon it was the coalition that crossed here first, in April. Here the Red Army is three months ahead.
