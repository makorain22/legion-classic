# Counter-stroke at Sokolovo

**August 1946.** The Germans slipped across the Seym in the night and took Sokolovo with a battalion and a few tanks. If they are allowed to dig in, the whole division will have to go round.

## Orders

- Retake Sokolovo.
- Hold it against the German counter-attack.
- Keep Polkovnik Gromov alive.
- Optional: keep at least three T-34s in the fight. The division will need them.

## Intelligence

- A battalion of Panzergrenadiers holds the village, with a bunker, sandbags, machine guns and one Panzer IV.
- Their armour will come down the east road soon after the village falls, and grenadiers by the north track after it.
- The rocket truck can shake the defenders out of the houses. Keep it well back.

> "Three years ago we took this village for the first time. Let it be the last." - Polkovnik Arkady Gromov
