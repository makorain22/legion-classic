# Crossing the Donets

**The Donets, April 1947.** With the army group gone from the Don, the Reich's southern front is falling back to its old river lines. The spring thaw has turned the valley to mud. The Reich holds the ridge on the west bank, with guns, trenches and wire.

## Orders

- Get across the Donets and hold the west bank.
- Break the ridge line.
- Hold the crossing against the Reich's counter-attack.
- Keep Podpolkovnik Zharkov alive.
- Optional: destroy the Reich base beyond the ridge.

## Intelligence

- There are two fords, north and south. The guns on the ridge cover both, and their howitzers will shell them.
- Mud slows wheels badly and tanks less. The road is the fastest way to the river.
- Their reserve will come down the west road with heavy tanks once the ridge falls.
- If the relief force was destroyed on the Myshkova, two IS tanks are with you; if the Sokolovo crews survived, two more T-34s.

> In the canon it was the coalition crossing beneath this ridge. Here it is the Red Army, and the ridge belongs to the Reich.
