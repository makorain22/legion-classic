# The Spree Forest

**South of Berlin, April 1948.** The 17th Guards Tank Brigade swings round Berlin from the south. When it reaches the Potsdam road, the city will be surrounded.

In the woods to the south-west, a pocket of the Free German Army - the coalition's soldiers - is fighting its way out of the regency's lines. Their commander has radioed that they will not fire on the Red Army.

## Orders

- Take the Baruth crossroads.
- Close the ring: reach the Potsdam road.
- Keep Podpolkovnik Zharkov alive.
- Optional: hold fire on the Free Germans and bring Podpolkovnik Zharkov to their pocket to parley.

## Intelligence

- The crossroads are held by bunkers, a gun, riflemen and two Panzer IVs. The regency's base is in the north-west, by the Potsdam road.
- The Free Germans will neither shoot nor be shot at unless provoked. If the parley succeeds they will fight beside you, here and in Berlin. If you fire on them, they will defend themselves.
- If the Narew road was cut, four more T-34s have come up.

> "We have the same enemy today. Tomorrow - the politicians can decide." - Major Falk, Free German Army
