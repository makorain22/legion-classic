# The Narew Winter

**The Narew, December 1947.** A blizzard over the Narew bridgehead. North of Warsaw the Reich's army has a single road west, and the 17th Guards Tank Brigade is going to sit on it.

## Orders

- Break the Reich line in front of the bridgehead.
- Take the town of Rozan.
- Cut the road west before the Reich's army escapes along it.
- Keep Podpolkovnik Zharkov alive.

## Intelligence

- The line has trenches, wire, four bunkers and a gun. The snow hides you as much as them.
- Rozan has bunkers and three Panzer IVs. Their base lies north of it.
- Once Rozan falls, the Reich will start pulling back. You have eight minutes to reach the road.
- If the Vistula bridgehead held, three more T-34s have come up.

> "Through the line, through the town, and west - fast." - Podpolkovnik Zharkov
