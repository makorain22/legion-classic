# Fortress Posen

**Poznań, January 1948.** The front has rolled past Posen and left it behind: a ring of brick forts built for another century, a citadel in the middle, and a garrison ordered to hold to the last man.

## Orders

- Take Fort Winiary, Fort Grolman and Fort Raczynski.
- Then take the Citadel.
- Keep Kapitan Nazarov alive.

## Intelligence

- Each fort is garrisoned, with a machine-gun nest, a gun and wire before it. Bring the guns up to the walls; send engineers with the storm groups.
- The Citadel has bunkers, a watchtower, a howitzer that will shell your start line, and a heavy tank.
- Expect a sortie from the east after five minutes.

> "Forts built for another century, and still they'll take a week each if we let them." - Kapitan Nazarov
