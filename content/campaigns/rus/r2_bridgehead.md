# The Bridgehead

**September 1946.** The division has reached the river. The bridges are down, but there are fords. Once across, the far bank must be held until the engineers bring up a construction truck.

## Orders

- Get ten men or vehicles onto the far bank.
- Hold the bridgehead for eight minutes.
- Then build a base and destroy Kampfgruppe Brandt.

## Intelligence

- Kampfgruppe Brandt has a base on the far side and will counter-attack as soon as you are across.
- Find the fords before you send the tanks. Infantry can wade where vehicles cannot.
