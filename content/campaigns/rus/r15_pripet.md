# The Pripet Marshes

**Polesia, September 1947.** Ninety kilometres of bog between the Reich's armies in the south and the north. The Reich watches the roads round it, not the paths through it. Nadezhda Orlova's partisans know those paths, and a whole rifle division is to follow them.

## Orders

- Take the three causeways, one after another.
- Ambush the reserves before they reach the columns.
- Bring the division's columns to the far side.
- Keep Nadezhda Orlova and the columns alive.

## Intelligence

- Each causeway has a machine-gun nest and riflemen; the second and third have bunkers. The columns move up only when a causeway is clear.
- Men can wade the pools; vehicles cannot. There is no base.
- Once the second causeway falls, the Reich will send its reserves up the north track, and later the south.
- If the partisans of Bryansk are still with you, eight more come with guides.

> "Your division won't get lost in the reeds, Comrade General. We were born in them." - Nadezhda Orlova
