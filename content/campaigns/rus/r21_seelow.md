# The Seelow Heights

**Seelow, March 1948.** Between the Oder and Berlin there is one last wall: the Seelow heights. Three trench belts climb the slope, with guns and heavy tanks behind them, and the town of Seelow sits on top. The 5th Guards Rifle Division attacks at night, with a hundred and forty searchlights in the defenders' eyes and the whole front's artillery behind it.

## Orders

- Break the first, second and third belts.
- Take Seelow.
- Keep Polkovnik Gromov alive.
- Optional: destroy the 9th Army base.

## Intelligence

- The barrage opens on the first belt and lifts as each belt breaks. Expect counter-attacks from the north and the south, with Tigers.
- Each belt has bunkers every twenty tiles and guns on the road. Their howitzers sit between the second and third belts.
- If Küstrin was taken, the bridgehead's rifle regiment joins you; if the guns of Posen were taken, two more batteries.

> "At the top there's nothing between us and Berlin." - Polkovnik Gromov
