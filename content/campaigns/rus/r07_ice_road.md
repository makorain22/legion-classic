# The Ice Road

**The Don, January 1947.** The lake north of the army group froze a week ago. Now it sends its lorries over the ice in convoys, north towards the Donets, to bring back fuel and shells for the pocket.

## Orders

- Stop the convoys: destroy four of them before they reach the north shore.
- Keep Podpolkovnik Zharkov alive.
- Optional: get a scout car to the hill over the lake, so the corps guns can shell the ice road.

## Intelligence

- Six convoys of four lorries will leave the crossroads south of the lake, each with a tank for escort. If three get through, the army group can hold out all winter.
- The ice carries men and lorries, but not tanks. Heavy shells and rocket salvoes break it, and whatever is on it goes through.
- A coalition rearguard holds the crossroads with a bunker, a gun and two tanks.

> "Then we catch them before the ice - or break it under them." - Podpolkovnik Zharkov
